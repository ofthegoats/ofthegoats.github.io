M.Sc.R. student at the University of Bristol [[https://plrg-bristol.github.io/][Programming Languages
Research Group]], supervised by [[https://seis.bristol.ac.uk/~tz20861/][Dr. Alex Kavvos]].

My research interests revolve primarily around:
- programming languages
- (categorical, modal, program) logic and semantics
- category theory and duality
- linear logic and concurrency

More generally, I am interested in the mathematical foundations of
computation and verification, especially through the lens of category
theory and logic.
