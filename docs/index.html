GitHub; ORCID; LinkedIn; Fediverse; CV

M.Sc.R. student at the University of Bristol Programming Languages Research Group, supervised by Dr. Alex Kavvos.

My research interests revolve primarily around:

  • programming languages
  • (categorical, modal, program) logic and semantics
  • category theory and duality
  • linear logic and concurrency

More generally, I am interested in the mathematical foundations of computation and verification, especially through the lens of category theory and logic.
